Type the grey words into Grab or Bolt. 🚻 toilets · 🪑 seats/sunbeds · 🚶 easy walking

Every day: rest in the condo 1–4 PM (hottest) · water + sunscreen + cap · passports stay in the condo · temples: shoulders and knees covered

Thu 26 Nov · Beach and rest (all within 10 min)

  • 10:00 Nai Harn Beach (5 min) · rent sunbeds + umbrella · swim only between the flags 🚻🪑🚶
  • Lunch at a beach restaurant → rest at the condo
  • 5:30 PM Promthep Cape for sunset (10 min, ~150–200 baht) · stay at the top viewpoint, skip the steps down 🚻🚶

Fri 27 Nov · Thai temples (private car with driver, ~5 hours)

  • 10:00 Wat Chalong · Phuket's biggest temple, flat · about 1 hour 🚻🚶
  • 11:30 Big Buddha Phuket · car goes to the top · short walk + a few steps · huge views 🚻
  • 1:00 PM lunch in Chalong · optional 1-hour Thai foot massage (~300–500 baht) 🪑

Keep the same car all day: Grab is hard to find at Big Buddha for the way back.

Sat 28 Nov · Beach day 2 (or a boat tour instead)

  • 10:00 Karon Viewpoint · famous 3-beaches photo, no walking
  • 10:30 Kata Noi Beach · quiet, beautiful, sunbeds + restaurants 🚻🪑🚶
  • Evening: walk around Nai Harn Lake (next to the condo, flat, benches) 🪑🚶

Sun 29 Nov · Old Phuket Town + Sunday market

  • 5–8 PM Sunday Walking Street on Thalang Road · street food, crafts, music · bags in front
  • 8:00 PM book the Grab home early (busy)
