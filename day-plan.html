Pure vegetarian

🍽️ Food worth trying clean, new, not Indian

Fri
Pure Vegan Heaven (Chalong, near Wat Chalong)Fully vegan, so no egg either. Try the sweet potato pancakes, smoothie bowls and sticky date toffee cake. Tripadvisor 4.6.
Sat
☕ Afternoon tea at The Shore at Katathani (Kata Noi)Sea-view afternoon tea with sweet and savoury treats, right at Kata Noi beach. Book a day ahead and ask for a vegetarian, egg-free set. Alternative: "Grande High Tea" in a wooden birdcage at Avista Grande, Karon.
Sun
Blue Elephant Phuket (Old Town)Royal Thai cuisine in a 100-year-old governor's mansion, with a vegetarian menu. A special-occasion dinner; book ahead. On Reddit: "amazing veg food".
Near home
Go Vegan Cafe (Rawai)Simple vegan café close to the condo for easy lunches.
Try
Thai dishes, the vegetarian wayPad Thai with tofu, green curry with tofu and vegetables, mango sticky rice, coconut ice cream, Thai iced tea.
💡 Look for a yellow flag with red letters "เจ" (jay): Thai vegan food, no meat, fish, egg or dairy. Show this when ordering anywhere: ไม่กินเนื้อสัตว์ ไม่ใส่น้ำปลา ไม่ใส่ไข่ ("No meat, no fish sauce, no egg").
