Sun 29 Nov · Day 4

Old Phuket Town + Sunday market

🚕 Taxi today: about ฿1,200–1,500 (≈ ₹3,100–3,900)
2:30 PM
📍 Tunk-ka Cafe on Khao Rang (Rang Hill) 🚻🪑🚕 ~฿500–650 from the condo. About 40 min. Cars drive to the top. Traditional Thai restaurant with the best view over Phuket Town. Not a vegetarian kitchen: order a Thai iced tea or coconut, or vegetable dishes using the phrase card (no meat, no fish sauce, no egg).
4:00 PM
📍 Soi Romanee 🚻🪑🚶🚕 ~฿120–150 from Rang Hill. Colourful Sino-Portuguese shophouses on Thalang Road. Flat; sit in cafés.
5–8 PM
Sunday Walking Street (free) Thai-Chinese culture, crafts and music, often local performances on the stage. Look for yellow "เจ" flags for vegetarian stalls; choose busy stalls that cook fresh in front of you. Keep bags in front.
Dinner
🌱 📍 The Vegan Table (Phuket Town)🚕 ~฿550–700 home to the condo after (evening, busy). 100% vegan, sit-down, clean and calm if the street stalls feel too much. Google 4.5 from 1,100+ reviews.
💡 Book the Grab home by 8 PM; it gets busy.
