Viewpoint, beach, sunset slow start, all within 25 min

Fri 27 Nov · Day 2

Big Buddha + optional temple and sunset

🚕 Taxi today: about ฿800–1,300₹2,100–3,400
10:00
📍 Big Buddha Phuket🚕 ~฿300–400₹780–1,000, about 30 min. The 45 m white marble Buddha on a hilltop, with views over the whole south of the island. Cars drive to the top car park; then a short walk and some steps. Shoulders and knees covered (sarongs at the entrance). Open about 8 AM to 5:30 PM; go in the morning.
Getting down
Plan the ride back before going upGrab drivers rarely wait at the top. Best: ask the same Grab driver to wait about 1 hour and bring you back, and agree the waiting fee first (about ฿200–300₹520–780 extra). Or take one of the taxis or tuk-tuks waiting in the car park (agree about ฿300–500₹780–1,300 to Chalong before getting in). Don't stay until evening: one Reddit user paid ฿985₹2,600 at 6:30 PM.
~12:00
Optional: 📍 Wat Chalong on the way downAbout 15–20 min from Big Buddha and on the road home. Phuket's most important temple, ornate and flat. Only if they still have energy; 30–45 min is enough.
~12:45
🌱 Lunch at 📍 Pure Vegan Heaven (Soi Ta-iad, next to Wat Chalong)100% vegan: sweet potato pancakes, smoothie bowls, sticky date toffee cake. About ฿150–300₹390–780 per dish. 🚕 ~฿250–350₹650–910 home after.
5:15 PM
🌅 Optional sunset: 📍 Promthep CapePhuket's most famous sunset, free, 10 min from the condo (🚕 ~฿150–200₹390–520 each way). Stay at the top viewpoint; skip the steps down. Too tired? Skip it.

Sun 29 Nov · Day 4

Old Phuket Town + Sunday market

🚕 Taxi today: about ฿1,200–1,500₹3,100–3,900
2:30 PM
📍 Tunk-ka Cafe on Khao Rang (Rang Hill)🚕 ~฿500–650₹1,300–1,700 from the condo. About 40 min. Cars drive to the top. Traditional Thai restaurant with the best view over Phuket Town. Not a vegetarian kitchen: order a Thai iced tea or coconut (about ฿80–120₹210–310), or vegetable dishes (about ฿200–350₹520–910) using the phrase card (no meat, no fish sauce, no egg).
4:00 PM
📍 Soi Romanee🚕 ~฿120–150₹310–390 from Rang Hill. Colourful Sino-Portuguese shophouses on Thalang Road. Flat; sit in cafés.
5–8 PM
Sunday Walking Street (free) Thai-Chinese culture, crafts and music, often local performances on the stage. Look for yellow "เจ" flags for vegetarian stalls; choose busy stalls that cook fresh in front of you. Keep bags in front.
Dinner
🌱 📍 The Vegan Table (Phuket Town)🚕 ~฿550–700₹1,400–1,800 home to the condo after (evening, busy). 100% vegan, sit-down, clean and calm if the street stalls feel too much. About ฿200–350₹520–910 per dish. Google 4.5 from 1,100+ reviews.
💡 Book the Grab home by 8 PM; it gets busy.
