✈️ First time flying? Step-by-step guideTap to open
🙋 Lost or unsure? Ask anyone in a uniform. In India: Air India staff (red/purple uniform) or the "May I help you" desk. In Phuket: anyone at an "Information" desk. Show your boarding pass and say where you need to go.

🏠 Leaving home

  1. Leave by 5:30 AM. Passports, printed folder, phones, cash, medicines in the cabin bag.
  2. Reach Bhopal (Raja Bhoj) Airport by 6:15 AM at the latest. Ask the driver for the Departures drop-off.

🛫 Bhopal airport

  1. Entrance gate: show your printed ticket + passport to the guard.
  2. Inside, find the Air India counter (look at the signboards above the counters).
  3. Give passports and the big suitcases. Say: "Final destination Phuket. Please check our bags through to Phuket and give boarding passes for both flights."
  4. Check the bag sticker says HKT (Phuket). Keep the small bag-tag stickers they give you: they are your proof for the bags.
  5. You get 2 boarding passes each: Bhopal→Delhi and Delhi→Phuket. Each shows the gate, boarding time and seat (for example 23A).
  6. Security: phones, power bank, keys, coins, belt and the clear liquids bag go in the tray. Women go through the separate ladies' line. Collect everything after.
  7. Look at the Departures screens for AI1886 and its gate number. Go to that gate and sit nearby. Toilets are close by; go before boarding.
  8. Boarding starts about 40 minutes before the flight. Show boarding pass + passport. Sometimes a bus takes you to the plane.
  9. On the plane, find your seat number (row number + letter). Small bag goes under the seat in front or in the overhead locker; ask the crew for help lifting it (backs!). Fasten the seat belt.

🔁 Delhi: change to the international flight

  1. Land at Terminal 2 about 9:45 AM. Follow the signs for "Transfer" / "International connections" / Terminal 3. If unsure, ask Air India staff: "International transfer to Terminal 3, flight to Phuket?"
  2. If the bags were checked through, do not wait for them. Only if Bhopal staff said "collect bags in Delhi": collect them at the belt shown on the screen, then check them in again at the Air India counter in Terminal 3.
  3. At Terminal 3 Departures entrance: show passport + boarding pass.
  4. Emigration (passport check for leaving India): show passport + boarding pass. If asked: "Tourism, Phuket, 8 days, coming back 2 December." The officer stamps your passport.
  5. Security again, same as Bhopal.
  6. Find AI2376 on the screens and walk to the gate. Terminal 3 gates can be a 10–15 minute walk: use the moving walkways and sit down on the way. Eat and buy water after security.
  7. Be at the gate by 1:00 PM. Flight leaves 1:40 PM.

🛬 On the flight (4 h 30 min)

  1. Meals: say "vegetarian, no egg" to the crew when they come.
  2. Walk to the toilet once or twice and stretch: good for the back.
  3. Before landing, change your watch: Thailand is 1 h 30 min ahead. Landing is about 7:45 PM Thai time.

🇹🇭 Phuket airport

  1. Phone on, Data Roaming ON (Mom and Papa). Simran: switch the Thailand eSIM on. WhatsApp the family: "Landed".
  2. Follow "Arrivals / Immigration". Choose the foreign passports line.
  3. Show passport + TDAC QR (printed or photo) + return ticket. They take a photo and fingerprints. If asked: "Tourism, 8 days, staying in Rawai."
  4. Baggage claim: the screens show the belt number for AI2376. Trolleys are free.
  5. Customs: walk through the green "Nothing to declare" exit.
  6. In the arrival hall go to Gate 2. Find the Klook sign with "SIMRAN GOSWAMI" and show the Klook voucher. Do not go with anyone else who offers a taxi.
  7. Can't find the driver after 20 minutes? Call Phuket Rainbow: +66 62 242 6288, or WhatsApp Rishabh.
  8. About 1 hour drive. The host Raissa (+66 99 237 3425) gives you the key. WhatsApp the family: "Reached". 🎉

Fri 27 Nov · Day 2

Big Buddha + optional temple and sunset

🚕 Taxi today: about ฿800–1,300₹2,100–3,400
10:00
📍 Big Buddha Phuket 🚻🚕 ~฿300–400₹780–1,000, about 30 min. The 45 m white marble Buddha on a hilltop, with views over the whole south of the island. Cars drive to the top car park; then a short walk and some steps. Shoulders and knees covered (sarongs at the entrance). Open about 8 AM to 5:30 PM; go in the morning.
Getting down
Plan the ride back before going upGrab drivers rarely wait at the top. Best: ask the same Grab driver to wait about 1 hour and bring you back, and agree the waiting fee first (about ฿200–300₹520–780 extra). Or take one of the taxis or tuk-tuks waiting in the car park (agree about ฿300–500₹780–1,300 to Chalong before getting in). Don't stay until evening: one Reddit user paid ฿985₹2,600 at 6:30 PM.
~12:00
Optional: 📍 Wat Chalong on the way down 🚻🚶About 15–20 min from Big Buddha and on the road home. Phuket's most important temple, ornate and flat. Only if they still have energy; 30–45 min is enough.
~12:45
🌱 Lunch at 📍 Pure Vegan Heaven (Soi Ta-iad, next to Wat Chalong)100% vegan: sweet potato pancakes, smoothie bowls, sticky date toffee cake. About ฿150–300₹390–780 per dish. 🚕 ~฿250–350₹650–910 home after.
